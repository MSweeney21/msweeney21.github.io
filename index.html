My mission

Matthew Sweeney, CFA

I’ve built Molokai Investments to help families focus on what matters most: faith and family. Having worked with family business owners, and now being one myself, I’ve seen how hard it is to balance faith, family, business, and the personal financial picture. My work brings your family’s values into every part of your financial life, whether that’s estate planning, legacy building, running the business, or investing itself.

As a Family CFO, I work with a small number of families at a time. I don’t keep the books, draft documents, or replace your current advisers. I read your financials, coordinate the people who do those things, and make sure you have one cohesive strategy across your family, your business, and your life.

My Family Value Portfolio is available to families who want their investments to reflect their values, as long as it fits their goals and circumstances.
