About

Matthew Sweeney, CFA

I grew up as the middle of five kids in Tallahassee, Florida. I attended Stetson University, where I earned a BS in mathematics and a BBA in finance while kicking for the university’s football team.

After graduating, I worked as a financial analyst at Luma Capital in Naples, Florida. During Covid, I moved to Raleigh to serve as finance director at Radeas, a medical laboratory. As I build Molokai, I also teach geometry at St. Thomas More Academy, a local Catholic school. Teaching has been a surprising source of joy for me over the past year and a half.

In Raleigh, I met my wife, Laurie, playing basketball with a group of Catholics in Cary. We were married in December 2024 and welcomed our first daughter in March 2026. Our wedding fell on the weekend of the Feast of the Holy Family, a fitting, if originally unrelated, connection to the mission of Molokai.

I was blessed to be raised Catholic and am involved in the faith community in the Raleigh–Durham area. We are parishioners at St. Catherine of Siena Catholic Church. In my free time, I enjoy playing and watching sports, taking walks with my family, reading, drawing, hiking, and traveling.
