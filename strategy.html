Five key tenets. Centered on family values.

Companies are filtered according to these standards, regardless of price. The screen is informed by the U.S. Conference of Catholic Bishops’ investing guidelines, papal teaching, and other Christian screening approaches.

Protecting human life

From natural conception to natural death.

Promoting the common good

Businesses that serve the good of all people.

Upholding human dignity

Every person valued, every person treated with respect.

Seeking economic justice

Fair wages, fair dealing, and honest work.

Protecting our home

Treating creation with responsibility.

Deep value investing.

I define risk as the chance of permanently losing money, not short-term swings. In the tradition of the great value investors, I look for:

Long-term focus

Valued on long-term outlooks, not short-term swings.

Misunderstood businesses

Out-of-favor companies the market has overlooked or misread.

Concentrated portfolio

Fewer than 25 holdings I understand, rebalanced by the size of the discount.

Small to mid cap

Smaller public companies, where careful research matters most.

Bought at a good price

Conservatively valued on its track record, bought only at a discount to that value.
